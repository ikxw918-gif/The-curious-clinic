ABOUT US

Curiosity belongs in medicine.

The Curious Clinic is an interactive clinical-reasoning project built to make learning medicine feel like stepping into a real clinic.

Instead of being given every answer, you decide what to ask, what to examine, and which investigations are worth ordering. Each case is fictional and designed for educational practice.

🧠 Think Connect symptoms and clues.
🧪 Investigate Order targeted tests.
🔎 Learn Understand why the diagnosis fits.
